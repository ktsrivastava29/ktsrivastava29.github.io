Delete your PaisaPebble account

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open PaisaPebble and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (top-right on the Home screen).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Option 2: Without the app

Email the developer address shown on PaisaPebble's Google Play page, from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my PaisaPebble account". We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, profile photo link) and preferences.
  • All personal records: transactions, accounts, categories, transfers, budgets and planned payments.
  • Your group memberships.

What is kept

  • Expenses and settlements you added to shared groups stay so the other members' balances remain correct. Your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and your email is removed.
  • Comments and receipt photos you added to shared groups remain visible to that group.

Nothing is kept for advertising or any other purpose. See the privacy policy for details.
