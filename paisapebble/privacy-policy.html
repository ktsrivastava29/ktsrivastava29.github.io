PaisaPebble Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

PaisaPebble ("the app", "we") is an Android app for tracking personal expenses, splitting group expenses and planning upcoming payments. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and the choices you have. In short: without an account, your data stays on your phone. If you sign in with Google, your data is stored in our cloud database so it can sync across your devices and be shared with the groups you join. We never sell your data.

PaisaPebble only records amounts you type in, or payments you choose to save when the optional payment detection (section 4a) asks you. It does not connect to your bank, UPI or any payment service, and it never moves money.

1. Using the app without an account

You can use PaisaPebble without signing in. Everything you enter — transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, planned payments, loans, savings goals and groups — is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. If you sign in with Google

Signing in lets you sync between devices and share groups with other people. When you sign in we receive from Google your name, email address and profile photo link. We do not receive your Google password or access to your other Google data (Gmail, Drive, contacts, etc.).

While you are signed in, the following is stored in our cloud database so it can sync:

  • your profile: display name, email, profile photo link, currency and date preferences;
  • personal records: transactions (amount, type, category, title, notes, date, account), including whether you marked a transaction private, accounts, categories, transfers between your accounts, budgets, planned / recurring payments with their status, loans you record (the person's or lender's name if you enter one, amount, dates, notes, and for loan apps or banks an optional loan number and interest rate) with their repayments, and savings goals with the amounts you add or take out;
  • groups you create or join: group name and settings, members, group expenses and how they were split, settlements, comments on expenses, an activity log (who added or changed what), and receipt photos that members attach to group expenses;
  • technical records needed for reliable sync (for example, which changes have been received).

Your personal records are visible only to you. Group information is visible only to the current members of that group; people removed from a group lose access.

3. Information about other people

When you add someone to a group, you enter their name and, optionally, their Gmail address. The Gmail address is used only so that, when that person joins with the same Google account, they are matched to their place in the group automatically. Only members of that group can see it. Please add only people who expect to share expenses with you.

4. What stays on your phone only

  • Receipt photos you attach to personal transactions.
  • The profile photo you choose inside the app.
  • Your notification, reminder and quiet-hours settings.
  • CSV exports, PDF reports and backup files you create — these are saved or shared only where you choose.
  • Receipt scanning: when you scan a receipt, the text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The photo is not uploaded for scanning.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock: unlocking private transactions uses Android's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock check. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN — it only learns whether the check passed.
  • Home screen widget: shows today's and this month's spending on your home screen (as dots if you turned on "hide amounts"). It is drawn on your phone.

4a. Payment detection (optional)

The app offers to turn on Auto-detect payments. Nothing is read until you agree and allow notification access for PaisaPebble in Android's settings. Once allowed, the app reads notifications from payment, bank and SMS apps (for example Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, bank apps and your SMS app) to spot payments you just made, such as "₹250 paid to Swiggy". It then asks you "Log ₹250 · Swiggy?" so you can save it with one tap.

  • Only notifications from those kinds of apps are looked at; all others are ignored.
  • Only the amount and the payee name are taken from a payment notification. The rest of the text — and any notification that is not an outgoing payment (OTPs, money received, offers, reminders, personal messages) — is ignored and not stored.
  • This happens entirely on your phone. Notification content is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • A payment is recorded only if you tap Save or Edit. It then becomes a normal transaction, marked as added automatically (synced like any other if you are signed in, section 2).
  • You can turn it off in Settings at any time, or remove the app's notification access in Android settings.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to sync when you are signed in, and to load ads.
  • Notifications: for reminders about planned payments, budget alerts and group activity. You can turn each kind off in Settings.
  • Run at startup: so reminders are restored after your phone restarts.
  • Notification access (optional): only if you turn on payment detection (section 4a).
  • Fingerprint / biometrics: to unlock private transactions with Android's own check (section 4).
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or pick a receipt or profile photo.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads (section 7).

6. Service providers

  • Supabase (privacy policy) hosts our database, sign-in service and file storage for group receipts. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and database rules ensure each user can only read their own data and their groups' data.
  • Google Sign-In (privacy policy) is used to sign you in.
  • Google AdMob shows ads (section 7).
  • Sentry (privacy policy) receives crash and error reports (section 7a).

We do not use analytics services, and we do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone for their own marketing.

7a. Crash and error reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the error type and where in the app's code it happened, the app version, device model, Android version, language, free memory, a short list of the screens visited just before the error, and for a small sample of sessions, how long some screens took to load.

Reports never contain your transactions, amounts, notes, names, group details, email address or account ID: numbers and quoted text are removed from error messages on your phone before sending, and no screenshots or screen recordings are taken. Reports are not used for advertising and are kept for at most 90 days. Sentry stores them in the United States.

7. Advertising

The app shows a small banner ad on the Insights and Calendar screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location (from the IP address), and device and app information. AdMob never receives your transactions, amounts, groups or any other content you enter. Google uses this data under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

8. How long we keep data

  • Synced data is kept while your account exists, so it can sync to your devices.
  • Group invitation links and codes expire automatically (links after 7 days, codes after 30 days by default).
  • When you delete your account (section 9), your personal data is deleted from our database.

9. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Delete account permanently deletes your profile, preferences and all personal records from our database and signs you out. Expenses and settlements you added to groups are kept so the other members' balances stay correct, but your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and your email is removed. Receipt photos and comments in groups remain visible to those groups.
  • Without the app: see how to request account deletion.
  • Signing out removes all PaisaPebble data from that phone. Without an account, uninstalling the app deletes everything.

10. Children

PaisaPebble is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, access is restricted by per-user database rules, and group invitation tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information. Lock screen notifications hide amounts by default.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: use the developer contact email on PaisaPebble's Google Play page.
