PaisaPebble

Small expenses. Clearer finances. Track income and expenses, split bills with friends and family, settle up, and plan rent, bills and EMIs with reminders — in one app, even offline.

  • Personal expenses, accounts, budgets and insights
  • Group expenses with equal, exact, percentage, share and item-wise splits
  • Simplified settle-up suggestions
  • Planned and recurring payments with reminders

PaisaPebble only records what you enter. It never moves money and never connects to your bank or UPI.
