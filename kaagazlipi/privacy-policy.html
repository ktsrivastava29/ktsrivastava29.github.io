KaagazLipi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

KaagazLipi ("the app", "we") is a PDF and document tool for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your documents never leave your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and sends an anonymous crash report when something goes wrong.

1. Your documents and photos stay on your device

  • Every PDF, image, scan, note, signature and drawing is processed on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any server.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in, and no cloud storage.
  • Files you save go to your phone's Downloads › KaagazLipi folder, or back to the original file if you choose "Replace original".
  • Drafts and working copies are kept in the app's private storage and deleted automatically after 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes them immediately.
  • The list of recent files is stored only on your phone.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you scan a document. Images stay on your phone.
  • Photos and files: used only for the files you choose to open.
  • Internet: used to load ads (section 4) and send anonymous crash reports (section 3). Your documents are never sent.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads (section 4).

3. Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a crash-reporting service, so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • the error message and the place in the app's code where it happened;
  • the app version, device model, Android version, screen size, language and free memory;
  • a short list of the app screens and actions just before the error;
  • for a small sample of sessions, how long some actions took (performance data).

A report never contains your documents, their contents, file names or folder paths (these are removed on your phone before sending), screenshots, your name, email, phone number, contacts or location. Reports are not linked to you, are not used for advertising, and are not sold or shared. Sentry stores them in the United States and deletes them after at most 90 days.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads on its Home, Files and result screens (never while you edit a document). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, and an app-specific ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location (from the IP address);
  • device and app information, and how you interact with the ads.

Google acts as a separate controller of this data under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. AdMob never receives your documents, their contents or file names.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads), which stops personalised ads.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Your choices

  • You can change ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices (where it applies) and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • You can delete the app's saved files from Downloads › KaagazLipi at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its private data, including drafts and recent files.
  • Because crash reports are anonymous, we cannot identify reports from a particular person. If you have questions, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer contact on KaagazLipi's Google Play page.
